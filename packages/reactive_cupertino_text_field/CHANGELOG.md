## [3.0.0]

### Breaking changes

- Migrate Material widgets from `package:flutter/material.dart` to
  `package:material_ui`.
- Raise the minimum Flutter version to `3.44.0` and the Dart SDK to `^3.12.0`.
- Require `reactive_forms` `>=19.0.0 <20.0.0`.
- Migrate Cupertino widgets to `package:cupertino_ui`.

### Fixes

- Replace deprecated `scribbleEnabled` with `stylusHandwritingEnabled`.
